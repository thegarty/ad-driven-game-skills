Date: 26 October 2026. Scenarios are defined in [the evaluation set](../scenarios.json).
Independent agents received the scenario requests, relevant skill folders and
read-only constraints. They were told not to read the rubrics, this result file,
the release validation document or other agents' responses. The following is a
sanitised summary of their actual outputs, assessed against the rubrics after
completion; it is not a transcript of private agent context.

## Correction found during evaluation

The initial conceptual reward-settlement pseudocode returned early for an
already-applied claim, which could leave a pending receipt unacknowledged. The
surrounding text expected that receipt to be cleared. The primary reviewer
corrected the pseudocode, and the independent evaluator re-read the correction
before completing its sequence assessment. This is an evaluated revision, not
a claim that the initial text was flawless.

The size evaluation began while its inventory-helper documentation was still
being authored, so that resource was initially unavailable. It is included in
the completed package and covered by link validation. The evaluator also
identified limited update-specific guidance; the entry point now explicitly
requires measuring actual update transfers and distinguishes them from archive
growth. The core size/store/compression answer did not depend on the missing
in-flight helper document.

## Verification limits

The independent ad evaluator supplied an original, reproducible
[fake-provider/SQLite design model](../harnesses/ad_reward_model.py). Its 15 checks
were rerun from this repository and passed. They exercise local state and
transaction contracts, including no-fill, callback order, duplicates, privacy
generations and restart after injected exceptions. They do not import Godot or
an advertising SDK; simulated crashes are exceptions plus database reopen, not
power-loss tests.

These are individual behavioral exercises, not a large statistical model
benchmark. The results do not prove every agent will always select or follow the
skills. Design models and arithmetic checks do not establish Godot/native SDK
correctness, actual store acceptance, human retention or revenue uplift.
No real ads, purchases, campaigns or game releases were performed.
