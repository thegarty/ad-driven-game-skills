# Evidence for engagement-oriented interface work

Distinguish four kinds of support: platform guidance, research in a particular
setting, observations in this game and a proposed design hypothesis. A design
guideline can justify a test without proving a revenue or retention effect.

Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski's 2006 studies connect perceived competence and
autonomy with enjoyment and interest in continued play. Use this as a reason to
test clear choices and understandable feedback, not as proof that adding a
progress bar causes mobile retention. The studies were not tests of this
game's ad-funded interface. [Original paper](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf).

Tyack and Mekler's 2024 review cautions against shallow applications of
self-determination theory in games HCI. Naming a motivation construct does not
validate a feature or a psychological mechanism. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12639).
Sources checked 2026-10-26. These are research rationales with context limits,
not a universal engagement formula or an assertion of dopamine release.

## From idea to falsifiable test

Synthetic example: players leave after losing a run. A proposed result screen
shows an earned upgrade and previews its effect. Hypothesis: more players can
identify a useful next action and voluntarily start another run. Alternatives:
difficulty, crashes, ad interruption, fatigue or a complete session may explain
leaving. Do not infer confusion from every exit.

First observe unfamiliar players performing the task without coaching. Record
whether they correctly explain the choice, apply it, recover from mistakes and
recognise the free continue route. Think-aloud sessions reveal issues; their
timings are not clean population performance estimates. Then test a stable
variant with assignment, exposure, outcome windows and guardrails specified
before examining results.

Useful measures: unassisted task success, wrong/accidental taps, recovery, time
to a meaningful action, voluntary replay, reported enjoyment and mature return
cohorts. Define active play separately from loading, menu dwell, idle waiting
and ads. Longer sessions can signal either engagement or confusion. Include
frustration/abandonment, accessibility, purchase misunderstanding, crash and ad
complaint guardrails. Never combine these into an unexplained engagement score.

Record what was tested: revision, screen, participants, device, locale, method,
sample limits, result, alternative explanations and next decision. A generated
mockup is **proposed**; a UI test is **source-tested**; a player observation is
measured in its stated setting; a controlled live experiment still needs valid
assignment and analysis. Report inconclusive results as inconclusive.
