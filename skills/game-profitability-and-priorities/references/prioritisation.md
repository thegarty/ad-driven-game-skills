# Prioritising game economics

## Keep the units aligned

For a stated cohort horizon:

`publisher ad revenue/install + net purchase revenue/install - variable cost/install = contribution/install before acquisition`

Subtract acquisition cost per install for contribution after acquisition. Use publisher-realised ad proceeds rather than advertiser gross spend. If eCPM already represents publisher proceeds, do not deduct the network's share again. Define purchase refunds, taxes and store fees consistently. Fixed operating/content costs still need coverage.

Break down ad proceeds into retained active days × impressions per active day × realised eCPM / 1,000 only when the averages describe compatible populations and windows. This simplification can hide correlation between retention and yield; use user/cohort revenue directly when available.

## Decision strength

| Evidence | Supports | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Source inspection | plausible defect, missing instrumentation | frequency of occurrence or commercial impact |
| Source tests | behaviour under tested conditions | native delivery, live retention or revenue |
| Simulation | consequences of explicit model assumptions | human choice, real ad supply or actual retention |
| Measured live descriptive data | observed rates and revenue in a defined window | causal effect without a suitable comparison |
| Controlled live experiment | effect for the tested design/population within uncertainty | automatic generalisation to other genres or markets |

## Overgrowth-derived example

The 25 October 2026 review found recoverable optional-ad failures and progression confusion, with source tests and synthetic gameplay comparisons. Its provider observation was too small and its retention coverage too incomplete to justify a profitable acquisition forecast. This supports fixing observable failures and completing measurement before increasing ad pressure. It does not prove that the fixes increased retention or earnings.

Use original synthetic fixtures to exercise the same reasoning. Preserve time, audience, source build and model assumptions whenever importing a lesson from another game.
