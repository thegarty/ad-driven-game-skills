# Game profitability and priorities

Translate evidence into the next concrete decision. Establish the game's stage, current constraint and authorised resources. Inspect source reports and definitions; do not treat a polished dashboard or a large test count as a measured commercial result.

## Build a comparable economic picture

Use one cohort, currency, horizon and population at a time. Combine publisher ad proceeds, net purchase proceeds and variable servicing costs. Keep acquisition spend separate so contribution before and after acquisition are both visible. Report fixed/content/operating costs separately; a positive marginal cohort does not establish company profitability.

Retained active days and impressions per active day help explain revenue, but geography, format, fill and seasonality can change yield. Do not extrapolate a tiny sample eCPM into a lifetime guarantee. Avoid multiplying different populations, such as all-install revenue by consented-user retention, without explicitly modelling that mismatch.

Use [the revenue sensitivity helper](references/revenue-helper.md) for explicit scenarios. Its arithmetic does not estimate causal uplift, infer an unobserved retention tail, calculate statistical confidence or authorise acquisition spend.

## Find the binding constraint

- If players cannot reliably start, save or receive earned rewards, prioritise correctness and recovery.
- If eligible offers rarely become visible or available, diagnose the funnel before adding frequency.
- If players stall or misunderstand the next goal, investigate strategy discovery and progression clarity.
- If value falls despite higher daily ad revenue, compare mature cohort retention and cumulative contribution.
- If measurement is missing, estimate the value of resolving the uncertainty before proposing a large product or spending change.

Read the [decision framework](references/prioritisation.md) when comparing competing interventions. Use the [priority ledger](assets/priority-ledger.md) to keep evidence, confidence, implementation cost and next verification together. Scores may assist ordering; they do not turn uncertain inputs into objective facts.

## Recommend a sequence

Rank a short set of changes by expected mechanism, evidence strength, effort, reversibility and downside. Separate fixes, product hypotheses, instrumentation and commercial tests. Give each item an owner-ready next action and an acceptance criterion. Respect the selected product and monetisation model; do not introduce purchases, forced ads or a slower free path merely because they might increase one metric.

Deliver a decision memo with the evidence window, calculation definitions, highest-priority actions and what would change the recommendation. Clearly label source-inspected, source-tested, simulated and measured-live claims. Forecasts remain forecasts until comparable live cohorts support them.
