# Game growth and acquisition

Connect the game's real appeal to the players most likely to enjoy it and return. Inspect the live product, available creative rights, store assets, analytics and campaign state before recommending a launch or budget change.

## Establish what is ready

Identify audience, markets, platforms, genre, differentiation, acquisition channels and spending constraints. Check whether the advertised experience exists in the shipped build. Verify store destination, onboarding, crash/reward failures and attribution before paying to send more users through the funnel.

Separate three questions: does the creative attract interest, does the store convert qualified visits, and do acquired players create enough retained value? Cheap installs alone do not answer the last question.

Use [soft-launch gates](references/soft-launch.md) and the [scorecard](assets/soft-launch-scorecard.md) to turn technical readiness, product learning, retention and economics into explicit continue/revise/stop decisions with a bounded learning budget.

## Design creative and store tests

Build a small concept matrix: audience motivation × real gameplay moment × opening hook × clear payoff. Change one interpretable element per comparison when possible. Show authentic interaction, understandable stakes and the actual visual style. A playable should teach a truthful sample of the loop and end with a clear store action.

Write a production brief containing aspect ratios/placements to verify with the selected network, footage/assets required, copy, variants and success criteria. Use captions and readable UI; check that the opening works without sound. Review asset licences and any generated-media rights for the intended use.

Align icon, screenshots, preview and description with the same promise. Use store-specific experiments where available and compare their defined populations, not incomparable console percentages. See [store and attribution references](references/growth-recipes.md).

For production work, follow the [creative pipeline](references/creative-pipeline.md) and [test brief](assets/creative-test-brief.md): preserve authentic footage, separate concept from execution hypotheses, verify export specifications/rights, and connect creative IDs to mature player outcomes.

## Measure acquired player quality

Use the [acquisition measurement plan](assets/acquisition-plan.md). Compare install cohorts by source, creative, platform and geography through the same maturity window. Keep acquisition spend, publisher ad earnings and optional purchase proceeds in their own ledgers.

Document attribution windows, redownload treatment, consent coverage, modelling and delays. Deduplicate across network/MMP/store reports before aggregating. Missing attribution is unknown; do not silently label every unmatched install organic. Assess incrementality with an appropriate holdout when scale justifies it.

Use observed cohort revenue and cost through a declared horizon to evaluate payback. Label any later lifetime value as a projection with assumptions and sensitivity. A higher eCPM in one country does not prove that country has profitable acquisition.

## Deliver

Provide a prioritised creative/store backlog, measurement gaps, a bounded test proposal and explicit stop/continue criteria. Specify spend and campaign mutations only within the user's existing authorisation. If data is insufficient, deliver the next learning test with its cost ceiling and evidence goal rather than inventing a profitable CPI.
