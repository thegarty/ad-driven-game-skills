# Store, creative and attribution recipes

## A bounded first acquisition test

Start with one interpretable product promise and a small set of distinct creative hypotheses. Confirm the spending ceiling and permitted campaign actions. Validate store links, install/open measurement and the first playable session before exposure. A technical readiness test can establish that tracking works; its sample may be too small to establish campaign economics.

Keep a creative ID in the campaign reporting join, not personal details in the game. Analyse the promise-to-experience mismatch: high click response with weak activation can indicate misleading expectations, poor loading/onboarding or a mismatched audience. Investigate before buying more volume.

## Official sources

Verified 2026-10-26. Recheck these for the selected account, region and platform before configuring a test; availability and interfaces change.

- [Apple product page optimisation](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/) — store page test capability and product-page context.
- [Google Play store listing experiments](https://play.google.com/console/about/store-listing-experiments/) — store listing experiment workflow.
- [Apple ad attribution](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ad-attribution/) — privacy-preserving campaign attribution and postbacks. Limited or delayed postback detail cannot be interpreted as complete user-level history.

## Reporting boundaries

Keep platform attribution, analytics acquisition dimensions and campaign network reports separately labelled until their windows and populations match. Document modelled conversions, re-engagement, redownloads and timezone differences. When a campaign starts after a tracking repair, use matched post-repair cohorts rather than claiming an uplift against missing historical events.

For an ad-funded game, acquisition economics depend on retained active days, realised publisher revenue and the entire player's cost. Synthetic eCPM × impressions arithmetic is useful for sensitivity testing, not a spending instruction.
