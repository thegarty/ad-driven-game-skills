# A repeatable acquisition creative pipeline

Produce a small interpretable set of authentic game promises, then learn which brings players who enjoy the shipped game. This is original workflow guidance. Network formats, placements and reporting change; verify the selected network's official specification and record its URL/date in every production brief.

## Concept before variation

Start with a specific player motivation and a real playable moment: solving a spatial puzzle, recovering a failing defense, discovering a production chain or making a clever upgrade choice. Connect opening action, stakes, feedback and payoff to the game's actual first-session experience.

Distinguish **concept changes** (different appeal or player motivation) from **execution changes** (caption, cut, opening frame). Ten near-identical captions do not test ten concepts. Use a stable creative family ID and version IDs so the data can answer both questions.

## Production handoff

Use the [creative test brief](../assets/creative-test-brief.md). It should contain:

1. Audience/motivation and the specific promise, with the build/scene that substantiates it.
2. A shot list: opening interaction, visible objective, decision, consequence and payoff. For a playable, include input hints, failure/success states and the install destination.
3. Capture instructions: clean test save, reproducible scene/state, HUD visibility, device orientation and permission to use every asset, font, voice and music cue. Preserve original footage so re-edits do not require another gameplay capture.
4. Verified format constraints: duration, resolution/aspect ratio, safe regions, file/container/codec/byte limits, captions, audio and store destination. Keep a high-quality master and compressed delivery variants; do not confuse acquisition creative limits with app binary size limits.
5. One interpretable hypothesis per variation when practical; label deliberate multi-factor concept tests as such rather than attributing results to one caption.

## Quality gate

Compare the final export to actual play. Reject misleading mechanics, impossible rewards, unreadable captions, fabricated hand gestures, unsupported claims and fake system UI. Check sound-off comprehension, contrast, safe cropping, language, end-card destination and install/open flow on a target device. Verify licensed/generated assets for the intended commercial use. A visually attractive ad that promises another game can reduce acquired-player quality.

Maintain a compact inventory of source project, master, delivery variant, rights record, approved copy, specification date, destination, status and retirement reason. Remove unused production intermediates from the app build; marketing files do not belong in its export by default.

## Test in layers

First verify delivery/tracking with a bounded technical exercise. Then compare concepts within as comparable a platform/market/audience/time population as the campaign system allows. Campaign allocation can be adaptive and nonrandom; its reported creative differences are not automatically causal A/B estimates.

Record spend, reach, frequency, clicks, store visits, attributed installs, activation, mature retention and net cohort value using each source's definition. High click response plus weak activation suggests a promise, audience, store or onboarding mismatch. Low CPI plus weak retained value is not a winner. In sparse data, use creative diagnostics to select the next hypothesis without claiming profitable scale.

For store pages, align the same promise across icon, screenshots, video and description. Use [Apple's product-page experiments](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/) or [Play listing experiments](https://play.google.com/console/about/store-listing-experiments/) where available; sources checked **2026-10-26**. Inspect native eligibility, test populations and metric definitions before comparing them with paid campaign reports.

## Refresh based on evidence

When performance falls, inspect audience saturation/frequency, placement/market mix, auction changes, store or game releases, tracking breaks and creative age. A decline is not automatically fatigue. Keep a known reference creative, maintain a hypothesis log and retire variants with a reason. Re-edit a successful concept only when the new test answers a question; do not generate endless assets without a measurement or deployment plan.
